Aon Client Leadership

As we deepen our focus on client‑led growth, enabling colleagues to create more value by applying the objectives of Aon Client Leadership—understanding client priorities, broadening strategic conversations, and collaborating across Aon to deliver stronger outcomes.

Colleagues will be rewarded for referring opportunities throughout Risk Capital and Human Capital Solutions, driving growth through meaningful client value delivery. By collaborating and recording eligible referrals on our DAU Portal or Aon Connect, you can earn incentives and prizes when introductions lead to new client business.

A successful internal referral
can earn you…
  • R1 000 per sale across all Solution Lines where the annual revenue to Aon is less than R50 000 per annum.
  • 10% of the revenue (up to a maximum of R25 000) across all Solution Lines where the annual revenue to Aon is R50 000 or more per annum.
  • A quarterly award of 10 000 Aon Stars for the colleague who achieved the highest number of sales.
  • An annual award of 100 000 Aon Stars for the colleague who achieved the highest number of sales for the year.
  • The winning team for the specific quarter will enjoy a team prize to the value of 50 000 at the end of the quarter.
